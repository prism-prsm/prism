SLIDE 04 — OBSERVATIONS

Asked at the bench

Is the free share really free?

Yes — claimFree() costs only the network gas. One per wallet, 500 in total, and the contract enforces both limits itself.

What happens to unsold shares?

They are burned. After 23 Nov 21:30 WIB anyone can call finalize(), which destroys every PRSM still held by the contract.

Can the terms change?

No. The price, caps and window are constants in a verified, immutable contract. There is no owner who could change them.

When does it end?

The window closes 23 Nov 2026 at 21:30 WIB — seven days after it opens. The wavelength scale at the bottom of this page tracks what is left.
